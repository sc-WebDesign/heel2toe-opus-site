## Why these two, and not the obvious ones

**The whole cobbling trade defaults to warm brown leather and gold**, and this
build refused that palette on purpose: it runs near-black with a lime accent.
Most shoe-repair stock photography is the same warm artisan frame, and three
strong candidates were dropped precisely because their own descriptions called
them that. **A photograph everybody else is using undoes a deliberate design
decision.**

- **`sole-work.jpg`** is a sole being set on a machine, and it is red, blue and
  green rather than sepia. It is the top service on the listing.
- **`key-cutting.jpg`** is a key blade being filed. It was chosen for the four
  trades section instead of a shoe, because **keys are the trade a passer-by
  does not know this shop does**, and a picture of a shoe there would have said
  what the sign already says.

## Checks made

- **The marks on the sole were enlarged and read.** They are the cobbler's own
  pen marks, a black cross and a red circle, not a maker's name. **The site
  names no footwear brand and neither does the photograph.**
- No faces in either frame. Nobody is named on this site and no staff count is
  claimed, so a portrait would be answering a question the copy does not.
- Nothing showing a zip. **Zip repair is not claimed anywhere**, because the only
  evidence for it is a review from nine years ago.
